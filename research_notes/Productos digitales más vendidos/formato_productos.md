# Anatomy of Top-Selling Digital Products and Their Listings / Sales Pages (by product type)

Research date: 2026-10-14. Sources are mostly 2025–2026. Fetching full pages failed (DNS errors for notion.com, gumroad.com, listifyai.net), so most findings come from search-result extracts. Official help pages (Etsy, Notion, Gumroad) are flagged as such; everything else is a vendor or creator blog and should be read as practitioner opinion.

## 1. Deliverable format: what each product type must contain (files, structure, inclusions, quality standards)

### Takeaway
Across all types, the deliverable that gets good reviews is "file or link + how-to-use instructions + license + support contact". Link-based products (Notion, Canva, Google Sheets) are delivered as a 1-page branded PDF containing the access link, because Etsy cannot deliver links directly. Platform rules set hard constraints: Notion requires instructions and standalone pages; Canva Pro elements can only be sold as template links; Etsy caps uploads at 5 files of 20MB each.

### Cited Findings
**Notion templates**
- Notion Marketplace (official): templates must include clear instructions (call-outs, videos, or placeholder content for customization), must be intuitive for new and existing Notion users, and must be submitted as standalone pages, not database subpages. Templates that collect personal information must explain how the data is used. They must respect copyright/trademark and Notion's Brand Guidelines. Paid templates require Notion approval, a payment-processor account, an approved creator profile, and ongoing buyer support. Notion may charge transaction or listing fees — [Notion Marketplace guidelines & terms](https://www.notion.com/sv/help/template-gallery-guidelines-and-terms)
- Notion's gallery application asks creators to define the target audience and what makes the template different, and to include demos, samples and instructions — [Gridfiti](https://gridfiti.com/notion-marketplaces/)
- Third-party marketplace Notion Everything asks for usage instructions and product visuals. Reported terms are a 20% affiliate fee via Gumroad and about 14 days to respond (single blog source) — [Gridfiti](https://gridfiti.com/notion-marketplaces/)
- Recommended delivery bundle: a PDF with the duplicate link, duplication instructions, and terms that forbid reselling or publicly sharing the link. Publish settings: keep "Allow duplicate as template" on, and turn off comments and search indexing — [Bardeen](https://www.bardeen.ai/answers/how-to-sell-notion-templates); [Bullet.so](https://bullet.so/blog/how-to-sell-notion-templates/); [Fungies](https://fungies.io/how-to-sell-notion-templates-online-3/)
- Send the "Duplicate" link only after a successful purchase. It can be automated by email (Resend/Postmark) — [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/sell-notion-templates)

**Canva templates**
- Templates that contain Canva Pro elements can only be shared as Canva template links. They cannot be sold as PDFs or other files, flattened or not. Templates made only from free elements and your own photos can be sold as a PDF or as a link. Minimal edits to an existing Canva template are not allowed. An e-book is not a "template" under Canva's definition, even with fill-in sections. Free or Pro content cannot be resold as stock or clipart — [Gelato](https://www.gelato.com/blog/how-to-sell-canva-templates-on-etsy); [AdNabu](https://blog.adnabu.com/etsy/how-to-sell-canva-templates-on-etsy/); [Brenda Cadman](https://brendacadman.com/can-i-sell-canva-designs-on-etsy/) (secondary sources paraphrasing Canva's "Rules for selling templates")
- Standard Etsy delivery is an instant-download PDF with the template hyperlink. The sources do not say whether this fully satisfies the link-only rule for Pro content, so treat it as a gray area — [Gelato](https://www.gelato.com/blog/how-to-sell-canva-templates-on-etsy)

**Google Sheets / Excel templates**
- You cannot sell a Google Sheets link directly on Etsy. Deliver a PDF that contains the link. To build the link: set sharing to "Anyone with the link can view", then replace "/edit?usp=sharing" with "/copy". This forces buyers to make their own copy and keeps the master safe — [The Bricks](https://www.thebricks.com/resources/guide-how-to-sell-google-sheets-on-etsy-using-ai)
- The PDF should contain a thank-you note, a hyperlinked call to action with the force-copy link, brief instructions, and support/FAQ contact — [The Bricks](https://www.thebricks.com/resources/guide-how-to-sell-google-sheets-on-etsy-using-ai)
- Troubleshooting section used by top shops: use File > Make a copy, sign in to a Google account, open on a computer rather than a phone, and make sure there is Drive space. The Etsy app does not support direct file downloads, so buyers need a browser — [TheSimpleSystemsShop (Etsy)](https://www.etsy.com/shop/TheSimpleSystemsShop); [TopSheets (Etsy)](https://www.etsy.com/shop/TopSheets)
- Creative Market Sheets listings tell buyers to click "Make a copy". Their license bans reselling, sublicensing, or giving away templates or modified versions — [Creative Market](https://creativemarket.com/BRANDcontent/281875842-Daily-Checklist-Google-Sheets)

**Printables / digital planners**
- A planner for GoodNotes must be a hyperlinked PDF. At minimum, link the top navigation tabs to their sections and the monthly calendar dates to their daily or weekly pages. Test every link in the actual app before listing — [Promptless Press](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-sell-digital-planners-etsy)
- Large-scale benchmark: one top Etsy planner advertises 600+ pages and 16,000+ hyperlinks, with a flip-through video on YouTube — [Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/835436485)

**Etsy file constraints (all downloadable types)**
- Up to 5 files per digital listing, 20MB maximum each (official) — [Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628347-How-to-Add-Files-to-a-Digital-Listing)
- A ZIP counts as one file but must still be 20MB or less. Larger products are split into multi-part archives, or hosted externally with a link in a PDF (community suggestion, not policy) — [Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Digital-File-Size-Uploads/m-p/140953043/highlight/true); [TheSTLSmith](https://thestlsmith.helpsite.com/articles/72561-why-i-have-to-use-multi-part-archives)

**Ebooks / guides**
- Typical format is a 30–80 page designed PDF with 5–7 chapters. Each chapter teaches one concept and ends with an action step — [Inkfluence AI](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/how-to-sell-fitness-ebooks) (AI-tool vendor; low confidence)
- PDF is the most compatible format. Adding EPUB/MOBI improves the reader experience, and Gumroad allows multiple files per product — [Top Bubble Index](https://www.topbubbleindex.com/blog/selling-ebooks-gumroad)

**Mini-courses**
- Easygenerator analyzed 3,802 courses and about 730,747 learner results (June 2025–June 2026). Completion was 85.5% for courses under 5 minutes and 70.6% for courses over 15 minutes, and it fell at each length step — [Easygenerator](https://www.easygenerator.com/en/blog/e-learning/how-long-should-an-elearning-course-be/)
- Recommended mini-course formats: 3–6 lessons priced at $29–79 ([Teachery](https://www.teachery.co/blog/how-long-should-online-course-be)); video lessons of 6–12 minutes, with activities outperforming lecture-only courses ([Ruzuku](https://www.ruzuku.com/learn/articles/how-long-should-online-course-be)); lessons of 5–10 minutes ([SchoolMaker](https://schoolmaker.com/blog/online-course-lengths))
- Teachery cites completion of 65–75% for 6–10 lessons, 35–45% for 11–20 lessons, and 15–25% for 21+ lessons. No methodology is given, and Teachery's own posts conflict with each other — [Teachery](https://www.teachery.co/blog/how-many-lessons-online-course)

**AI prompt packs**
- Prompt counts vary by source: 25–100 prompts by niche ([Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/sell-ai-prompts-online)), or 10–50 around one theme or workflow ([SpreadSimple](https://spreadsimple.com/blog/how-to-sell-ai-prompts-online/))
- Each prompt should have usage instructions and an example output, with consistent formatting and a clear topic and audience. Listings should be backed by demo outputs and clear licensing — [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/sell-ai-prompts-online); [TipSeason](https://tipseason.beehiiv.com/p/how-to-build-and-sell-ai-prompt-packs-on-gumroad-and-make-passive-income-while-you-sleep)
- Live packs are delivered as a PDF (for example, 22 pages), an Excel file, or a Notion/Google Docs link. Typical price is $15–49 — [Gumroad listing](https://aipromptsx.gumroad.com/l/anpsgo); [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/sell-ai-prompts-online)
- Niche test: "This pack saves [person] from having to [specific task]" — [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/sell-ai-prompts-online)

**Productized services**
- Define deliverables by what the client receives, not by your effort. State what is and is not included. Example: "Three preliminary logo designs… one final logo" with defined file and font outputs — [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/blog/productized-service)
- Spell out the number of revisions, the timeline, and the exclusions — [Shiprocket](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/productised-services/)

### Inferences
- Minimum "deliverable kit" checklist that applies to every type: (1) core file or access link; (2) a "START HERE" PDF with access steps, troubleshooting, and support email; (3) license terms (personal vs. commercial, no resale, no sharing the link); (4) for templates, a short video walkthrough or placeholder/example content. This matches Notion's official instruction requirement and the Sheets/Canva PDF-link pattern.
- Link-based products need anti-sharing protections: force-copy links, duplicate-only Notion pages with comments and indexing off, and license language. Without them the master can be edited or leaked.
- For mini-courses, the data favors short, action-oriented lessons (under 10 minutes, 3–6 lessons) to maximize completion, which correlates with satisfaction and fewer refunds. The refund link is an inference, not measured.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch Canva's official "Rules for selling templates" page. Canva rules are from secondary sources.
- No official Excel/Microsoft guidance on selling .xlsx templates was found. Common practice (deliver .xlsx plus an instructions PDF and protect formula cells) is unsourced here.
- Found no sourced quality standards for printables (DPI, A4 vs. US Letter sizing). These are commonly cited (300 DPI, both sizes) but were not verified in this research.
- No data was found on membership or community deliverable structure beyond the Skool notes in section 3.

## 2. Listing format: titles, thumbnails/mockups, descriptions, tags/SEO (Etsy & Gumroad), preview videos

### Takeaway
On Etsy, use the full image allotment (up to 20 photos plus one 5–15 second silent video). The first image must be square or landscape with margin for cropping. Titles have a 140-character cap with 13 tags of up to 20 characters each, and keywords go first. On Gumroad, use a 600x600+ thumbnail, cover images that show the outcome, a benefit-led scannable description, and Discover tags. Discover only lists a product after its first real sale, with a category set and ratings enabled.

### Cited Findings
**Etsy images and video**
- Up to 20 photos and 1 video of 5–15 seconds per listing (official help page; written for physical items, and the same editor is used for digital) — [Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-Listing-a-Physical-Item)
- Videos up to 100MB, audio is stripped (plays silently). Third-party claims of 2 videos or a 3-second minimum conflict with the official page — [Alura](https://alura.io/docs/article/etsy-listing-videos-guide)
- Seller Handbook (quoted by eRank, Nov 2025): the first photo should be horizontal or square so the subject survives thumbnail cropping, and it sets the shape for the other photos. Leave enough border to crop to a square. Etsy began testing vertical photos on some devices in summer 2025 — [eRank PDF](https://help.erank.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/photo-guidelines-for-etsy-listings.pdf)
- Size advice conflicts: 2000px short side ([Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/etsy-listing-photo-size-complete-guide-2025-seller-edition)) vs. a 1000px minimum ([Gelato](https://www.gelato.com/blog/etsy-product-photo-size)). Use sRGB. Anecdote: one seller cut preview images from 2400px to 1600px across 40 digital listings, halved page load, and saw sales rise 12% (not a controlled test) — [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/optimization-growth-strategies/how-to-etsy-listing-photo-size-2025-what-actually-works-after-testing-thousands-of-images)

**Etsy titles and tags**
- Title cap is 140 characters. 13 tags maximum, 20 characters each — [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/etsy-character-limit/); [TypeCount](https://typecount.com/blog/etsy-character-limit)
- Length advice conflicts: 120–140 characters, because titles under 80 underperform ([TypeCount](https://typecount.com/blog/etsy-character-limit)) vs. under 15 words / 60–80 characters ([ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-title-mistake-costing-you-sales); [Alura](https://alura.io/docs/article/optimizing-etsy-listing-titles)). ListifyAI claims Etsy rewrote its title guidance in April 2026 (state the item once; front-load attributes). Not verified with Etsy.
- Consensus: front-load the main keyword, because search results truncate titles — [Alura](https://alura.io/docs/article/optimizing-etsy-listing-titles)

**Gumroad listing**
- Thumbnails (shown in Library, Discover, and profile) should be at least 600x600px. Cover images are what visitors see first on the product page (official) — [Gumroad Help](https://gumroad.com/help/article/60-adding-a-cover-image)
- Recommended cover size: 2160x1350 (16:10) (unverified third party) — [Top Bubble Index](https://www.topbubbleindex.com/blog/selling-ebooks-gumroad)
- Text on covers must stay readable at thumbnail size. Show the outcome, not just the product. Descriptions should be benefit-led, with headings, bullets and bold. Avoid outbound links. Show templates or software in use with screenshots. Match messaging to the ad, post or email the visitor came from — [Kupkaike](https://kupkaike.com/blog/gumroad-product-page-best-practices); [Homeworking Henry](https://homeworkinghenry.com/how-i-am-going-to-optimise-my-gumroad/)
- Gumroad Discover (official): needs payout settings and at least $10 in genuine sales on the account. Each product needs at least 1 successful non-refunded sale, a category, and ratings enabled. Tags are added in Share > Gumroad Discover. Tag the way customers would search. Product pages include structured data and social meta tags — [Gumroad Help: Discover](https://gumroad.com/help/article/79-gumroad-discover); [Gumroad docs mirror](https://mintlify.com/antiwork/gumroad/features/products)

**Notion template listing visuals**
- Cover image: a clean Notion screenshot with a text overlay, plus a demo video of about 60 seconds — [Fungies](https://fungies.io/how-to-sell-notion-templates-online-3/); [Bullet.so](https://bullet.so/blog/how-to-sell-notion-templates/)

**Planner listing visuals**
- A flip-through video showing navigation is standard among top planner listings — [Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/835436485)

### Inferences
- Etsy digital image stack, synthesized: (1) hero mockup with a benefit headline, legible as a square crop; (2) "what's included" grid with page or tab count; (3–8) close-ups of key pages or dashboards on device mockups (iPad, laptop, phone); (9) how-it-works or delivery steps ("you'll receive a PDF with link"); (10) compatibility (GoodNotes/Notability, free Notion plan, Google account, Canva free); (11) size/format specs; (12) reviews or social proof; plus the silent 5–15 second scroll-through video. This is a synthesis, not an official spec.
- Title formula consistent with sources: [Primary keyword product] + [platform/format] + [key attribute/use case] + [audience], front-loaded, for example "Budget Spreadsheet Google Sheets, Monthly Budget Planner, Paycheck Tracker". The 2026 guidance trend favors fewer repetitions.
- Use all 13 tags with multi-word long-tail phrases that do not just repeat the title (common practitioner advice, not explicitly sourced above).

### Gaps
- Could not access Etsy's 2026 title guidance directly to confirm the reported April 2026 change.
- No Creative Market preview-image spec was found. The searched "1820x1214" spec could not be verified.
- No controlled data was found on the optimal number of mockup images for digital listings.

## 3. Sales page elements that convert best (headline, mockups, bonuses, guarantees, testimonials, FAQ) and benchmarks

### Takeaway
The consistent sales-page anatomy is: outcome headline → who it's for → mockups/preview (TOC and sample pages for ebooks; demo video for templates) → what's included / curriculum → bonuses → testimonials → transparent price and repeated CTAs → risk-reversal guarantee → FAQ. Hard conversion data for digital products specifically is scarce. General e-commerce averages about 1.8–2.1%, and "good" is about 3–5%.

### Cited Findings
- Benchmarks: global e-commerce conversion is about 2.1% (early 2025), UK 2.3% ([Smart Insights](https://www.smartinsights.com/ecommerce/ecommerce-analytics/ecommerce-conversion-rates/)). About 1.8% across about 18,000 stores; about 3.2% is "decent" and 4.8%+ is top performer ([Triple Whale](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/ecommerce-benchmarks)). Lead-gen landing pages: SaaS about 3.8%, legal up to 12.3% (Unbounce data via [SEOteric](https://www.seoteric.com/what-counts-as-a-good-conversion-rate-in-2025/)). None of these isolate digital products.
- A vendor claims optimized Gumroad pages convert at 4–8% (unverified) — [Kupkaike](https://kupkaike.com/blog/gumroad-product-page-best-practices)
- Guarantee: in an A/B test, a "60-day money-back guarantee" framed as risk reversal lifted sales by 29%, against +10% for a value-reminder variant (single agency case) — [Conversion Rate Experts](https://conversion-rate-experts.com/button-microcopy-win-report/)
- 14- or 30-day guarantees cost little in refunds, typically under 3% (vendor claim). Specific, conditional guarantees beat generic ones. Show the price openly, because hiding it erodes trust. Repeat CTAs down the page — [SchoolMaker](https://schoolmaker.com/blog/course-sales-page); [Teachable](https://teachable.com/blog/how-to-optimize-your-sales-page-for-conversions)
- Testimonials are called the most powerful conversion driver. 3–5 specific testimonials are claimed to lift conversion 15–30% (unsourced vendor claim) — [SchoolMaker](https://schoolmaker.com/course-creation-guide/how-to-write-online-course-sales-page-that-converts); [LearnyBox](https://learnybox.com/en/blog/how-to-create-sales-page-for-online-courses/)
- Ebook sales page: a hook, a table-of-contents preview, 2–3 sample pages as images, a "what you'll learn" bullet list, and testimonials — [Top Bubble Index](https://www.topbubbleindex.com/blog/selling-ebooks-gumroad)
- Membership/community (Skool About page): visitors should grasp who it's for and what changes for them within about 5 seconds. Most of 15 reviewed About pages failed at this (copywriter review) — [Skool Copy Hub](https://www.skool.com/skool-copy-hub-6921/i-reviewed-15-skool-about-pages-almost-all-were-missing-this). The "7 elements" of an About page are cover image, description, video sales letter, image tiles, and social proof — [Udemy course outline](https://www.udemy.com/course/skool-community-101/). A free community with valuable content works as a lead magnet for the paid tier (anecdotal) — [Sal's notes](https://salsnotes.notion.site/April-109b0cafe52380e6a4fccc9fdadf1206)
- Productized service page: a homepage plus pricing tables (tiers for entry point and upsell), a process section ("how it works"), project or portfolio pages, and an intake form instead of a contact form — [Framer template](https://framer.com/templates/delivr?via=framax); [Dorik](https://dorik.com/template/servicex-productized-service-website-template); [ManyRequests](https://www.manyrequests.com/blog/productized-consulting)

### Inferences
- Sales-page section order to use as a checklist: 1) headline with the specific outcome and audience; 2) subheadline with the mechanism or format; 3) hero mockup or demo video; 4) problem → solution; 5) "What's inside" with counts (pages, lessons, prompts, tabs); 6) preview (sample pages, TOC, screenshots, curriculum); 7) bonuses with stated value; 8) testimonials or reviews with specifics; 9) price, plus anchoring and tiers where relevant; 10) guarantee (specific, time-bound); 11) FAQ (compatibility, delivery, refunds, license, device requirements); 12) final CTA.
- The FAQ should pre-empt the main refund triggers seen in section 4: "Do I need Notion Plus or Canva Pro?", "Does it work on iPad or phone?", "How do I receive it?", "Is it physical?".

### Gaps
- No rigorous, digital-product-specific conversion benchmarks (Gumroad, Etsy digital, Stan Store) were found from a primary source.
- No sourced data was found on the conversion impact of bonuses or FAQ sections specifically.
- No membership conversion benchmarks were found.

## 4. Delivery mechanics, licensing, and common refund-causing mistakes

### Takeaway
Etsy delivers files (5 × 20MB) through the buyer's Purchases page, so link-based products ship as a PDF containing the link. Gumroad allows multiple files and custom refund policies, which show on the product page and are used in disputes. Digital sales are generally non-returnable for change of mind, but "not as described" or non-delivery cases still succeed. The main refund causes are listing/file mismatch, inaccessible links, and device or app incompatibility.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy (official): digital listings cannot be returned or cancelled, but buyers may be covered by Purchase Protection if the item is not as described or the file is never delivered — [Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115013328108-Downloading-a-Digital-Item)
- Community and blog reports agree: the no-refund notice only covers change of mind, and "not as described" cases succeed only when the file genuinely differs from the listing. The listing is the main evidence in disputes. A blog claims a new Etsy Cases Policy took effect in July 2026 (unverified) — [Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Refund-of-digital-good/m-p/143298800/highlight/true); [ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-digital-not-as-described-refunds)
- Broken or non-working download links lead to refund requests — [Etsy Community thread](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Customer-is-requesting-Refund-since-the-Etsy-links-to-download-a/td-p/146885670)
- The Etsy app does not support direct file downloads, so buyers must use a browser — [TopSheets (Etsy)](https://www.etsy.com/shop/TopSheets)
- Gumroad (official): custom refund policy is set under Product > Settings. It is displayed on the product page and included in card/PayPal dispute evidence — [Gumroad Help](https://gumroad.com/help/article/335-custom-refund-policy); [Gumroad blog](https://gumroad.com/blog/p/specify-a-refund-policy-for-your-products). Many prompt-pack listings state "all sales final" — [Gumroad listing](https://aipromptsx.gumroad.com/l/anpsgo)
- Licensing: Creative Market templates forbid reselling, sublicensing, or free redistribution of templates or modifications — [Creative Market](https://creativemarket.com/BRANDcontent/281875842-Daily-Checklist-Google-Sheets). Notion template bundles should include terms that forbid resale or public link sharing — [Bardeen](https://www.bardeen.ai/answers/how-to-sell-notion-templates). Canva Pro content cannot be resold as flattened files or stock — [Gelato](https://www.gelato.com/blog/how-to-sell-canva-templates-on-etsy)
- Notion paid templates require ongoing buyer support (official) — [Notion guidelines](https://www.notion.com/sv/help/template-gallery-guidelines-and-terms)
- Untested hyperlinks in planners are a quality failure. Test all links in GoodNotes before listing — [Promptless Press](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-sell-digital-planners-etsy)
- Platform fees cited: Gumroad 10% + processing (third party) — [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/sell-notion-templates)

### Inferences
Common refund and bad-review causes, synthesized as a pre-launch checklist:
1. The listing shows more than the file delivers (mockup pages not included, or a different page count). Make the images match the file exactly.
2. Buyer thinks it is a physical item. State "DIGITAL DOWNLOAD, nothing will be shipped" in the first image and the first description line.
3. Links not accessible: wrong sharing setting, an "/edit" link instead of "/copy", a Notion page not published or without duplicate enabled, or an expired link. Test from a logged-out or incognito account.
4. App/plan incompatibility: needs Canva Pro or Notion Plus, Excel features missing in Google Sheets, or a planner not tested on Notability/Xodo. Declare requirements up front.
5. Buyers trying to download in the Etsy app. Instruct them to use a browser.
6. No instructions or support, which causes confusion and refunds. Include a START HERE PDF, a walkthrough video, and a support email.
7. License ambiguity. State personal vs. commercial use, the number of users or seats, and no resale or redistribution. Commercial-use licenses are often sold as a separate higher tier (common practice; not sourced above).

### Gaps
- No quantitative refund-rate benchmarks by product type were found. The only figure is the vendor claim of under 3% for course guarantees.
- No official Gumroad default refund window was confirmed. Third-party claims of 30 or 60 days conflict and appear to be low-quality sources.
- Course-platform delivery mechanics (Teachable, Kajabi, Podia drip and access settings) and membership billing and cancellation norms were not researched in depth, given the tool-call budget.
