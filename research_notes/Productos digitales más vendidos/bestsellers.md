# Best-selling digital product categories and top products (2025–2026)

> Method note: these notes come from about 16 web searches run on 2026-10-14. WebFetch failed with DNS errors (ENOTFOUND) on insightraider.com, outfy.com and revenuegeeks.com, so most findings are based on search-result summaries and could not be checked against the full pages. Most sources are vendor, affiliate or tool blogs, not official marketplace data. Treat the numbers as directional unless a finding is marked as a primary or company figure.

## 1. Which digital product categories sell best in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
The categories named again and again across marketplaces are:
- printables and planners
- templates (Canva, Notion, spreadsheets)
- wall art
- social media and branding kits
- courses and memberships

On Etsy, printables, templates and wall art lead by volume. The most revenue goes to software/dev tools (on Gumroad) and to paid communities and memberships (Whop, Skool). On Hotmart, info-products and courses dominate, especially in finance/business, health/fitness and personal development.

### Cited Findings
- **Etsy:** Outfy's 2026 list of 31 top-selling Etsy digital products includes wall art printables, social media templates, digital planners and branding kits. — [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/top-selling-digital-products-on-etsy/)
- **Etsy:** InsightAgent says digital downloads are the top Etsy category by volume, led by printable planners, wall art and wedding stationery. It adds that the most-sold items are not always the most profitable. — [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/most-sold-items-etsy)
- **Etsy:** eRank's June 2026 top-keyword report lists "digital products", stickers, wall art and crochet pattern among Etsy's top search keywords. This measures search interest, not sales. — [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-june-2026/)
- **Etsy:** SellerToolsHQ ranks digital products first among Etsy categories because of their margins. The page says "Last updated: January 2025" (older data). — [SellerToolsHQ](https://sellertoolshq.com/guides/what-sells-best-on-etsy/)
- **Gumroad:** InsightRaider's modeled estimates for January–April 2026:
  - Software Development: about $65.8M across 1,083 products (about $60.8K per product)
  - Business & Money: about $15.4M across 1,500 products
  - 3D Assets: about $13.9M across 2,082 products
  - Method: ratings count × average price × a conversion multiplier. These are not Gumroad disclosures.
  — [InsightRaider Gumroad statistics 2026](https://insightraider.com/en/data/gumroad-statistics-2026); [State of Gumroad 2026](https://insightraider.com/es/state-of-gumroad-2026)
- **Stan Store:** More than 50% of creator income comes from simple digital downloads priced at $4–$30. Meetings (1:1 calls) and lightweight courses are the other major products. The source is undated. — [Sacra, Stan vs Whop](https://sacra.com/research/stan-vs-whop/)
- **Whop:** Paid Discord and Telegram access is the most common product. Trading signals, finance and trading communities have the highest revenue per subscriber. Whop's early fit came from sneaker bots, flipping tools, betting groups and day-trading memberships. — [BizToolKit (2026)](https://www.biztoolkit.co/post/whop-marketplace-seller-earnings-in-2026-what-creators-actually-make); [Sacra, Whop](https://sacra.com/c/whop)
- **Skool:** The communities with the highest estimated revenue are in money-making and business niches. Profitable.app's June 2026 modeled MRR estimates are an upper bound that assumes every member pays:
  - "Ai Agency Arbitrage": about $4.3M/month
  - "Wealthy Insurance Agent": about $1.36M/month
  - "Business Lending Masterclass": about $1.17M/month
  — [profitable.app Skool stats](https://profitable.app/skool/stats); [Skool revenue calculator](https://profitable.app/tools/skool-revenue-calculator)
- **Hotmart:** Third-party reports (Accio, in Portuguese) say finance and business lead in search volume and conversion, health, wellness and sports are steady sellers with high repeat purchases, and personal development leads in number of titles. Named bestsellers include "Emagrecer de Vez" (weight loss) and "Natural Bodybuilding". No sales figures are given. — [Accio, Hotmart ebooks mais vendidos](https://pt.accio.com/business/hotmart-ebooks-mais-vendidos); [Accio](https://pt.accio.com/business/ebooks-mais-vendidos-hotmart)
- **Udemy:** The Udemy 2026 Global Learning & Skills Trends Report (published 2025-09-18, data from 17,000+ enterprise customers) found:
  - GitHub Copilot learning up 13,534% year over year
  - Microsoft Copilot content up 3,400%
  - AI agents/agentic AI was the most-consumed new AI skill
  - Soft-skill learning up 25%, with critical thinking up 37% and decision-making up 38%
  — [Business Wire / Udemy](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250918925356/en); [Digital Terminal](https://digitalterminal.in/trending/udemy-report-reveals-ai-skills-as-fastest-growing-learning-category-globally)

### Inferences
- Bestsellers by volume (Etsy, Stan) are cheap downloads: printables, planners, templates and wall art. Bestsellers by revenue are high-priced or recurring products: software/dev tools, trading and finance communities, and "make money" or business masterminds on Skool and Whop.
- On course platforms (Udemy, and Hotmart in LatAm), AI skills and money/career topics lead demand.

### Gaps
- None of the platforms publishes an official ranking of digital product categories. Etsy, Gumroad, Payhip, Lemon Squeezy and Creative Market have no public bestseller-by-category reports that I could find.
- No data found for Creative Market, Payhip or Lemon Squeezy category performance.
- I could not open Udemy's "100 Fastest-Growing Global Skills for 2026" list ([link](https://business.udemy.com/tr/resources/2026-top-100-workplace-skills/)).

## 2. Specific bestselling products and creators with public revenue numbers

### Takeaway
Thomas Frank's Notion templates have the best-documented numbers: $1M+ self-reported by early 2023, and an estimated $2M+ in later secondary sources. Easlo is reported at about $500K. Platform-wide figures exist for Whop and Stan. Etsy shop figures are almost all EverBee estimates.

### Cited Findings
- **Thomas Frank (Notion templates):**
  - Self-reported passing $1M in template sales in an early-2023 thread. Template revenue more than offset drops in affiliate, sponsorship and Skillshare income. — [Typefully, Thomas Frank](https://typefully.com/TomFrankly/dollar1-million-in-notion-template-sales-kuFT0iD)
  - About $15K/month in 2021 from "Creator's Companion". A Feb 2026 case study estimates about $2.1M in template sales and about $120K/month average template revenue.
  - Top seller: Ultimate Brain at $129, about $760K+ in revenue.
  - The bundle reportedly outsells standalone products 3:1.
  - Other vendor guides claim $2.5M+, which is unverified.
  — [Foundry case study](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/thomas-frank-notion-templates-2m-creator-business); [Business Briefcase](https://businessbriefcase.substack.com/p/how-just-2-notion-templates-made)
- **Easlo (Jason Chin, Notion templates):**
  - Latka reports a $500K revenue run rate in 2023 with a one-person team. — [Latka](https://www.getlatka.com/companies/easlo.co)
  - Earlier figures: $0 to $27K in 8 months — [Goodreads repost](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/25863570-this-guy-has-earned-500k-selling-templates); over $200K by 2022 — [Fungies](https://fungies.io/how-to-sell-notion-templates.md); "over $300K in the last year" — [Nick Lafferty](https://nicklafferty.com/blog/sell-notion-templates/)
  - These are older data (2022–2023) and are not audited.
- **Whop (platform-wide):**
  - Average of $8,413/month per creator, and $845M in cumulative creator earnings as of Oct 2025. — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/whop)
  - Over $2B gross transaction volume and $142M ARR (Nov 2025). — [Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/note/whop-s-gen-z-earnings-platform-reaches-2b-gtv-and-142m-arr); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/whop-at-142m-revenue)
  - Sellers range from $500 to $500K+/month. The $500K+ figure for signal sellers is "reported", not verified. — [BizToolKit](https://www.biztoolkit.co/post/whop-marketplace-seller-earnings-in-2026-what-creators-actually-make)
- **Stan Store:**
  - More than 59K creators earned money on Stan in 2025. — [Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/report/stan)
  - About $28.3M ARR at end of 2024 (Sacra estimate) and about $35M estimated in 2025. — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/stan-vs-whop/); [Everything PR](https://everything-pr.com/stan-store-and-beacons-the-link-in-bio-platforms-that-became-creator-storefronts)
- **Skool:**
  - A May 2026 snapshot claims the top community runs at $5M+ per year. This is unverified. — [Communipass](https://communipass.com/?p=20194)
  - Skool Games leaderboards rank communities by MRR (live July 2026 per one source). Profitable.app says Skool does not publish revenue, which conflicts with that claim. — [SkoolPrep](https://skoolprep.com/skool-leaderboard-winners-traffic); [profitable.app](https://profitable.app/skool/stats)
- **Etsy Canva template and planner shops:**
  - EverBee-estimated planner shops: about $10.6K to $38.7K/month. — [Teachable course page](https://think-create-build-now.teachable.com/p/digital-planners-canva)
  - A 15-month-old Canva template shop is estimated at $9K/month, and a German tutorial claims $19K/month. — [Lilys.ai summaries](https://lilys.ai/de/notes/design-with-canva-20251118/canva-templates-etsy-tutorial)
  - A guide says top template shops make $20–30K+/month and typical successful shops $1–2K/month. — [FitSmallBusiness](https://fitsmallbusiness.com/how-to-sell-canva-templates-on-etsy/)
  - EverBee counts list price, not discounts, so it overstates revenue.
- **Gumroad:** The median creator earns about $72/month and the top 1% earn $10K+/month. The top 1% of creators reportedly capture 99.5% of revenue (modeled). — [InsightRaider](https://insightraider.com/en/data/gumroad-statistics-2026)
- **Canva Creators:** Royalties are paid on Pro usage of templates. The claimed $100M+ paid out is unconfirmed. A separate $200M AI Creator Compensation Program is not the template royalty pool. — [Startup Daily](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/artificial-intelligence-machine-learning/canva-earmarks-200-million-in-royalty-payments-for-new-ai-platform/); [Krea blog](https://www.krea.ai/blog/can-you-make-money-with-canva-creators-program-and-etsy-templates-in-2026)

### Inferences
- Revenue is highly concentrated on every platform. The typical creator earns little: the Gumroad median is about $72/month, and only about 2% of Whop products pass $1K/month per [BizToolKit](https://www.biztoolkit.co/post/whop-marketplace-seller-earnings-in-2026-what-creators-actually-make). The famous numbers come from creators who already had an audience (Thomas Frank's YouTube channel, Easlo's X/Twitter following).

### Gaps
- No verified 2025–2026 revenue disclosures were found for specific Etsy shops, Creative Market sellers, Payhip or Lemon Squeezy creators, or Canva Creator top earners.
- No public Gumroad "top products" list with sales counts was found.

## 3. Typical price ranges by category

### Takeaway
- Etsy printables and spreadsheets cluster under $10.
- Notion templates sell for about $19–79, with premium systems up to $129.
- Stan downloads run $4–30.
- Whop and Skool memberships run $10–500/month.
- Courses and info-products cost more (see gaps).

### Cited Findings
- **Etsy "google sheets planner":** median price $9, and 59% of listings are under $10 (keyword data Jan 2026). — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/google-sheets-planner)
- **Etsy budget spreadsheet example:** "Simple 2026 Budget Planner" (Excel/Sheets) at $5.96, down from $11.92. Findniche estimates of single budget-spreadsheet listings show very small sales, for example a debt payoff calculator at $5.04/month and a BrighterPlans budget planner at $2.88/month. — [Findniche](https://findniche.com/etsy-product/budget-planner-excel-google-sheets-budge-1807214506); [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy)
- **Notion templates:** the average successful template sells for $19–79 (per a vendor guide citing Gumroad and Notion marketplace data). Ultimate Brain sells at $129. — [Fungies](https://fungies.io/?p=37753); [Foundry](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/thomas-frank-notion-templates-2m-creator-business)
- **Stan Store downloads:** $4–30 make up most creator income. — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/stan-vs-whop/)
- **Whop:** Discord/Telegram communities at $10–50/month per subscriber, and trading signals at $50–500/month. — [BizToolKit](https://www.biztoolkit.co/post/whop-marketplace-seller-earnings-in-2026-what-creators-actually-make)
- **Skool:** pricing benchmarks for $5K, $10K and $50K/month communities exist in the Communipass 2026 article. I could not extract the details. — [Communipass](https://communipass.com/?p=20194)

### Inferences
- Under $10 is the norm for Etsy printables and sheets, so volume or bundles are needed. $19–129 is reachable for productivity "systems" with clear outcomes. Recurring $10–500/month memberships produce the largest revenue but need a community and an expert or "results" promise.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2025–2026 price-point data was found for Hotmart courses, Udemy course price distribution, presets/LUTs, AI prompt packs, or Creative Market assets.

## 4. Rising vs. saturated categories; best niches (including Spanish-speaking/LatAm)

### Takeaway
Generic printables, planners, budget sheets and plain Notion templates are crowded. Specific, niche-targeted versions, AI-skill education and AI workflow/prompt packs are described as rising, but the growth figures cited have no sources. LatAm is growing fast for Hotmart-style info-products. The strongest niches there are business/career, personal development, education, health/sport and finance (2021 data).

### Cited Findings
- **Generic products are saturated:**
  - Generic "daily planner"/"budget planner" Etsy listings are "a wall of identical grids". The advice is to target a specific user. — [Listadum (July 2026 data)](https://www.listadum.com/blog/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy)
  - Broad, generic downloads are harder to sell, and buyers "wanted faster wins", not more content. — [Solopreneur Code](https://solopreneurcode.substack.com/p/digital-products-2026); [ConversionProPlus](https://conversionproplus.com/blog/gumroad-trends-2026-what-s-selling-right-now)
- **Rising (unsourced claims):**
  - AI prompt/workflow packs are called the fastest-growing category of 2025, with "200%+ YoY" growth. — [ConversionProPlus](https://conversionproplus.com/blog/gumroad-trends-2026-what-s-selling-right-now); [Fungies niches](https://fungies.io/?p=40615)
  - Notion templates are cited at "+35% YoY". A niche Notion CRM for photographers reportedly outsells a generic CRM 10:1 (vendor quote).
- **AI skills demand (Udemy):** see the GitHub Copilot +13,534% and agentic-AI findings in section 1. — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250918925356/en)
- **LatAm and Hotmart:**
  - A Hotmart 2026 study on the creator economy in Spanish-speaking Latin America counts 250K+ creators, concentrated in Colombia. — [ImpactoTIC (Jan 2026)](https://impactotic.co/?p=114948)
  - Influencer income in LatAm grew 40% in the last year. — [Que.es (Jun 2026)](https://www.que.es/2026/06/19/economia-creadores-latam-hotmart/)
  - Hotmart projects the regional creator economy will grow 9× by 2033. International sales are 69% versus 31% local. — [DPL News](https://dplnews.com/?p=321143)
  - Digital product buyers in LatAm grew almost 3,000% (Hotmart; date not confirmed). — [DPL News](https://dplnews.com/?p=228050)
  - Hotmart reached 26M users at its 10th anniversary. — [Valora Analitik](https://www.valoraanalitik.com/?p=130447)
  - Top Colombian niches (2021, older data): business and career, personal development, education, health and sport, finance. — [Enter.co](https://www.enter.co/?p=510507)
  - The LatAm e-learning market is projected to grow from $30B (2024) to $52.1B by 2030, with Hotmart named among the leaders. — [Business Wire / ResearchAndMarkets](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20251023541770/en/Latin-America-E-Learning-Research-Report-2025-Market-to-Reach-%2452.1-Billion-by-2030-from-%2430-Billion-in-2024-with-Telefonica-Pearson-Anthology-Cogna-Educacao-and-Hotmart-Dominating---ResearchAndMarkets.com)
- **Whop and Skool:** finance, trading and "make money / AI agency" niches show the highest per-member revenue. — [BizToolKit](https://www.biztoolkit.co/post/whop-marketplace-seller-earnings-in-2026-what-creators-actually-make); [profitable.app](https://profitable.app/skool/stats)

### Inferences
- The opportunity is in specific versions of proven formats, such as a spreadsheet or Notion system for a defined profession, rather than new categories.
- Spanish-language versions of proven English products (budget sheets, business templates, AI guides) face less competition. LatAm buyers grow fast but have lower purchasing power, so Hotmart-style courses and ebooks in finance, business and personal development are the regional fit.

### Gaps
- No Google Trends time series or marketplace sales-count data was found to confirm "rising" or "saturated" claims.
- No 2025–2026 Hotmart official niche ranking was found. The 2026 study PDF was not accessible.
- No data was found on Etsy's AI-generated-content policy effects or on demand for presets/LUTs or digital stickers.

## 5. Which products Claude can realistically help create

### Takeaway
Claude can produce most of the text-, document-, spreadsheet- and code-based formats directly. It cannot produce finished photographic or graphic assets (presets/LUTs, illustrated stickers, polished wall art) without other tools.

### Cited Findings
- Vendor guides describe using AI for ebooks, prompt packs, templates and course content. — [Zenler](https://www.zenler.com/blog/ai-for-digital-products-creators); [LearnWithHasan](https://learnwithhasan.com/digital-product-ideas/?tag=dtc)

### Inferences (based on Claude's capabilities, not external sources)
- **High fit:**
  - spreadsheets (budget, debt payoff, business finance, inventory, Etsy/Shopify trackers) as .xlsx files with formulas
  - Notion template structure and content (databases, properties, written guides; the user must build or import them in Notion)
  - ebooks and guides, SOP and business-document packs (contracts, proposals, onboarding)
  - resume and cover-letter templates (.docx)
  - AI prompt packs and workflow guides
  - course outlines, scripts and worksheets
  - printable planners as PDF or HTML-to-PDF (functional, simple design)
  - code products (scripts, small SaaS tools, browser extensions), which match Gumroad's highest-revenue category, Software Development
  - Spanish-language versions of all of the above
- **Partial fit:** Canva templates (Claude can draft copy and layouts, but design is finished in Canva), community curricula for Skool or Whop (content yes, community operation no).
- **Low fit:** Lightroom presets/LUTs, illustrated stickers and wall art, 3D assets, fonts.

### Gaps
- No external data was found on how AI-made products perform compared with human-made ones on marketplaces.
